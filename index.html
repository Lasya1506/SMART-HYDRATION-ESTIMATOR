🧬 Sensor Fusion Algorithm

Our hydration estimation uses Sensor Fusion combining bio-impedance and skin temperature from the ESP32 wearable.


1. Bio-Impedance (Ω):
Water conducts electricity. Higher tissue impedance means less water. Values over 500Ω reduce the hydration score.


2. Temperature (°C):
Skin temp affects tissue resistance. We apply a thermal penalty if temp rises above 37.5°C, as heat causes sweating and compounds dehydration.


Calculation Formula:
Score = 100 - [(Imp - 500) × 0.1] - [(Temp - 37.5) × 10]
